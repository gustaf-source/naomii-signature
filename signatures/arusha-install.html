Install your Naomii email signature

One click to copy. Then paste into your Gmail signature settings and save.

  1. Click 📋 Copy signature below.
  2. Open Gmail → ⚙ → See all settings → General → scroll to Signature.
  3. Click your existing signature in the list, select all of the old version in the editor (Ctrl+A / Cmd+A), Delete, then Paste (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V).
  4. Scroll down and click Save Changes.
